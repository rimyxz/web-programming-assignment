About The Horror Archive

The Horror Archive is a website filled with horror stories, strange events, and unexplained mysteries. This site explores different topics related to horror such as urban legends, paranormal events, and unusual stories. It is for horror fans and anyone who enjoys exploring the unsettling and unknown.

I was motivated to create this site because stories about unexplained events are interesting to me. They leave room for people to create their own theories and interpretations. I thought it would be a fun topic to work with while also helping me practice my web programming skills.
